# Memo — Vireo Audio Support SLA

**To:** Neha Kulkarni, Support Operations Manager  
**Subject:** First-response SLA breach reduction

## What the data says
The support export contains 11,816 tickets. 2,555 missed their first-response target, a 21.62% breach rate. At Rs 350 per breach, the observed SLA-credit exposure is Rs 894,250.

The Morning shift is the main concentration: 2,103 breaches across 6,589 tickets, a 31.92% breach rate. Day is 8.49% and Night is 11.15%.

Chat is also the highest-breach channel at 27.05%, followed by Social at 22.03%, Email at 20.58%, and Voice at 5.75%.

## Recommended target
Reduce overall breach rate from 21.62% to 15%. At the observed ticket volume, that corresponds to approximately 783 fewer breaches and about Rs 274,050 less SLA-credit exposure if the Rs 350 credit remains unchanged.

## Action
Start with the Morning shift and inspect the highest-breach Tier 1 agents. Use a weekly agent/shift report to focus coaching and coverage changes rather than treating the entire desk as one group.

## Guardrail
This analysis identifies where breaches are concentrated; it does not prove why they occur. The next operational step should be a conversation with the relevant supervisors using the weekly agent and shift report.
